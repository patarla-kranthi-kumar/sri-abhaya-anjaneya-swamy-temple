About the temple

Sri Abhaya Anjaneya Swamy is Lord Hanuman as the giver of abhaya, freedom from fear. Devotees from Sreenivasarayunipalli and the villages around Madumuru come here to pray for courage, health and protection for their families.

The temple welcomes everyone. Visit for daily darshan, to offer prayers, or to take part in festivals with the community.

Prayers and offerings

Devotees commonly offer the following at Hanuman temples. To arrange any of them, please call the temple.

Archana

Prayer with your name and family star, offered by the priest.

Abhishekam

Ritual bathing of the deity with milk, water and sacred offerings.

Sindoor and vada mala

Traditional offerings to Lord Hanuman, especially on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Pradakshina

Circling the sanctum in prayer, often 11 or 108 times.

Days when Hanuman devotees traditionally gather. Contact the temple for this year's festival dates and programme.

Support the Temple Construction

🙏 Sevaye Madhava Seva 🙏

With the blessings of Lord Sri Abhaya Anjaneya Swamy, we hope to develop this temple into a welcoming spiritual place for devotees and the generations to come. At present, the temple is small, and limited funds have delayed further construction.

We are planning to build a Hanuman statue approximately 30–40 feet tall, along with the facilities needed for devotees. We warmly invite devotees, families, well-wishers, and anyone who wishes to support this effort to contribute according to their means. Every contribution, large or small, is appreciated.

All contributions received for this purpose will be set aside and saved until sufficient funds are available to begin the construction work. We also welcome visitors, suggestions, and guidance. Please contact us or visit the temple if you would like to learn more or offer support.
